## Design Context

### Users

ZuraAI is for privacy-conscious desktop users who want fast access to multiple AI providers, local models, research, and approval-gated agent workflows without giving up control of their data. Command Center users are keyboard-first and expect to move quickly between apps, chats, system actions, and first-party Zura tools.

### Brand Personality

Capable, composed, and personal. ZuraAI should feel like a polished desktop companion: confident enough to handle powerful workflows, calm enough to trust, and warm enough to avoid feeling like enterprise control software.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a refined, native-desktop visual language with dense information, crisp hierarchy, and restrained warmth. Preserve ZuraAI's dark acrylic Command Center palette, rose-tinted neutrals, flat menus, compact radii, and keyboard-first interaction model. Support both product themes elsewhere in the app, but treat Command Center as a focused OS overlay. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon AI gradients, decorative glass cards, and oversized marketing layouts.

### Design Principles

1. Optimize for momentum: primary actions and keyboard paths should remain obvious at a glance.
2. Keep power legible: reveal rich capability without making the interface feel dangerous or busy.
3. Earn every surface: prefer strong grouping, spacing, and typography over wrapping everything in cards.
4. Match the desktop: controls should feel native, compact, and responsive rather than web-page-like.
5. Preserve trust: clearly distinguish available behavior from previews, disabled capabilities, and actions that require approval.
