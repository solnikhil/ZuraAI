## Design Context

### Users
Desktop AI assistant users (developers, technical professionals, knowledge workers) who want multi-provider AI access, local control, and research capabilities. They're in their workflow, switching between models and tools, often for extended sessions.

### Brand Personality
**Professional + Efficient + Refined**

Clean, focused, polished — like a premium tool for power users. Not flashy, not bare-bones. The interface should feel capable and trustworthy without being cold or busy.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light mode, dark mode, and multiple theme presets supported
- Dark-first design (all themes are dark currently)
- Customizable accent colors let users personalize without losing cohesion
- Visual tone: structured, organized, clear hierarchy
- The Themes section should be a refined configuration panel — not a generic card grid, not an immersive visual picker, not flashy. Just thoughtful layout with breathing room and purpose.

### Anti-References
- **Overly minimal/sparse** — too much whitespace without purpose feels unfinished
- **Crammed density** — too many controls in one view without breathing room
- Generic card monotony where every section looks identical

### Design Principles

1. **Hierarchy through rhythm** — Use varied spacing to create clear groupings, not equal padding everywhere
2. **Structure over decoration** — Cards should group related content, not wrap every row identically
3. **Purposeful density** — Settings should feel comprehensive but never cramped; group related controls, leave space between groups
4. **Refined restraint** — Professional doesn't mean boring — type hierarchy, consistent spacing, and subtle depth create polish
5. **Functional clarity** — Every control is visible and actionable; no hidden affordances, no mystery icons
